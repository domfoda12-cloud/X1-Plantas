## Why it works (editor's read)

1. **Future-pacing hook.** "Three months from now, at this very time, you could be looking at the first room of your house, built by you." It makes the viewer picture the result in the first 5 seconds, with a concrete timeframe and a concrete outcome.
2. **Pain in two words.** "Sem diária" names the money the viewer is losing (paying a mason daily) without dwelling on it.
3. **Authority and claim.** "I created the most complete course" over footage of the creator doing the work proves she knows the trade.
4. **Show, don't tell, for the curriculum.** Every skill named on screen is shown at the same moment, so the list doubles as proof.
5. **Objection handling.** "Even if you've never done construction before" removes the beginner fear.
6. **Value stack, then price anchor.** The bonuses stack value, then "10, less than a Coke" makes the price feel trivial.
7. **Risk reversal.** "I send the course before you pay anything" removes the last objection.
8. **Low-friction CTA.** "Tap the button, message me, I'll send it." One action, sent on WhatsApp, where the audience already is.
9. **Format fit.** Raw phone footage and big captions look like normal content in the feed rather than an ad, and work with the sound off.

## Reusable ad template from this reference

1. **Hook (0–5 s):** "In [short timeframe], you could [specific, visual result], [done by you / without X]." Show the result or the process happening.
2. **Pain (1 line):** the cost or annoyance the viewer has now ("without paying a daily mason").
3. **Authority (1 line):** "I created [the most complete X]", over footage of you doing it.
4. **Access (1 line):** how easy it is to use ("straight from your phone").
5. **What they learn or get:** a fast list, one clip per item, cut on each word.
6. **Objection:** "even if you've never [done this] before."
7. **Value stack:** 3–4 bonuses, one cut each.
8. **Price anchor:** price compared to something trivial ("less than a Coke").
9. **Risk reversal:** "you get it before you pay" (or a guarantee).
10. **CTA:** a single action ("tap the button below and message me").

Production rules: 9:16 vertical, under 60 s, a cut every 0.5–1.5 s, captions on every line, real hands-on footage, no slow intro or logo at the start.
